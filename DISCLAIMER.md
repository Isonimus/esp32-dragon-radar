## Playing safely

The game places its seven targets at random points around you. It knows nothing about
what is at those points: a target can land on private property, a road, a railway, in
water or anywhere else you should not go. You are responsible for where you walk.
Respect private property and local laws, watch the traffic rather than the screen, and
skip any target you cannot reach safely.
